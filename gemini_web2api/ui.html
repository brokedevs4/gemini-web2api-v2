Google Gemini Session Cookies
Step 1
How to get your cookies (Choose Method A or B):
⚡ Method A (Fastest & Easiest): Using Cookie-Editor Extension
  1. Go to gemini.google.com in your browser.
  2. Click the Cookie-Editor extension icon in your toolbar.
  3. Click Export at the bottom → choose either Header String or JSON.
  4. Paste directly into the text box below (both formats are auto-detected)!
🛠️ Method B: Using Chrome DevTools (No Extension)
  1. Press F12 on gemini.google.com → go to Application → Cookies.
  2. Copy the values or full header string.
